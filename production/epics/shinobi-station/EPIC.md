# Epic: Shinobi Station

> **Goal**: An isometric hidden ninja village lives out its day on one screen while you watch: a steam train pulls into the station, kids play tag, ninjas train and sprint over the rooftops, and lanterns light up at dusk. Built in Godot 4.7.2 (GDScript) from the Aseprite sprites, matching the browser prototype in `prototypes/shinobi-station/`.
> **Brief**: `design/game-brief.md`
> **Scope**: the brief's Features list, in its Build order
> **Not in scope**: gameplay of any kind (no goal, money, building or losing), several districts, placing buildings or track
